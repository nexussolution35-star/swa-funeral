Privacy Policy

Who We Are

Funeral Parlor (Giyani, Limpopo) is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website and our branches.

Information We Collect

Names, contact details, ID numbers and dependant details that you give us when you enquire, join a plan or arrange a funeral.

How We Use It

To respond to enquiries, administer funeral plans and claims, arrange funerals and meet our legal obligations.

Sharing

We share information only with parties who need it to provide our services, such as the insurer underwriting a plan, or where the law requires it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and keep it only as long as needed.

Your Rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to its use. You may also complain to the Information Regulator.
