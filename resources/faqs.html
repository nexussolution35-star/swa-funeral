FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a funeral plan?

SMS the word “Swa” to 064 544 1292. A consultant will call you back.

How much does a plan cost?

Plans range from R160 to R390 per month, depending on the tier. Silver Plan B is R220 per month.

Who can I cover?

The main member, spouse, children and extended family. Silver Plan B covers the main member plus up to 15 dependants.

Is there a waiting period?

Yes, a 3-month waiting period applies to standard plans.

What is included?

Depending on the tier: casket, hearse and family car, tent and chairs, graveside décor, grocery benefit and, on selected tiers, a free tombstone.

What do I do when someone passes away?

Call us any time on 064 544 1292. We will collect your loved one and guide you through the next steps.

Where are your branches?

Giyani (head office), Phalaborwa, Sekgosese, Botlokwa, Saselamani and Soweto.

Still Have a Question?

Contact us and we will gladly help.
